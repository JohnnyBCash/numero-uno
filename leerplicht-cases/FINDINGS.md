# Findings — Leerplichtwet art. 5 onder b (richtingbezwaar)

Synthesis of the 23-case corpus in [`cases/`](cases/). Covers the legal
framework, the **maximum punishment**, the test courts actually apply, what
gets people acquitted vs. convicted, and the sentences imposed in practice.

> Scope note: this is a synthesis of public case law for research, not legal
> advice. Every claim links to an ECLI in the corpus; read the source before
> relying on it.

---

## 1. The offence and the maximum punishment

**The offence.** Under **art. 2(1) Leerplichtwet 1969 (Lpw)** the person
exercising authority over a minor (a parent/guardian) must ensure the child is
registered at — and attends — a school, *unless a statutory exemption applies*.
Failing to register is the typical charge in this corpus. It is an
**overtreding** (minor offence), tried by the **kantonrechter**.

**Maximum punishment — art. 26(1) Lpw** (quoted verbatim in
[GHAMS:2020:1670](cases/ECLI-NL-GHAMS-2020-1670.md)):

> *"De in artikel 2, eerste lid, bedoelde personen die de … opgelegde
> verplichting niet nakomen worden gestraft met een **hechtenis van ten hoogste
> een maand of geldboete van de tweede categorie**."*

So the statutory ceiling is:

| | Maximum |
|---|---|
| Custodial (hechtenis) | **1 month** |
| Fine (geldboete, 2nd category) | **≈ €4,500** (the 2nd-category amount, indexed periodically) |

These are alternatives, not cumulative. Note the offence is committed **per
child per school year**, so a parent of several children, or across multiple
years, can face repeated prosecutions.

## 2. The three statutory exemption grounds (art. 5 Lpw)

- **5(a)** — physical/psychological grounds (doctor's statement). *Not* the
  topic here; see the peripheral case [GHARL:2015:5814](cases/ECLI-NL-GHARL-2015-5814.md).
- **5(b)** — **richtingbezwaar**: "overwegende bedenkingen tegen de richting
  van het onderwijs" on *all* schools within reasonable distance. **The focus
  of this corpus.**
- **5(c)** — child registered at a school abroad; see peripheral case
  [RBSHE:2011:BP2276](cases/ECLI-NL-RBSHE-2011-BP2276.md).

## 3. The art. 5(b) test the courts apply

**"Richting"** means *a fundamental orientation derived from a particular
religious conviction or philosophy of life* (welbepaalde godsdienstige
overtuiging of levensbeschouwing) — [HR:2010:BL6719](cases/ECLI-NL-HR-2010-BL6719.md),
[HR:2018:1071](cases/ECLI-NL-HR-2018-1071.md).

The Hoge Raad's **three cumulative requirements** for valid "overwegende
bedenkingen", restated in [GHARL:2024:2205](cases/ECLI-NL-GHARL-2024-2205.md):

1. **(i)** serious conscientious objections (ernstige gewetensbezwaren) resting
   on a *welbepaalde* religious conviction or philosophy of life;
2. **(ii)** the objections concern the **richting** — the fundamental
   orientation — of the education in question;
3. **(iii)** they are **sufficiently concrete and sufficiently weighty**,
   connected to education as a school can actually provide.

**Expressly excluded** (do *not* qualify): objections to the *type* of education,
to compulsory education as such, or to the statutory organisation of education.
Objection to the *absence* of any worldview (i.e. objecting to neutral public
schools) *can* qualify.

### The "8(2) bar" — once chosen, stays chosen
Art. 8(2) Lpw blocks a 5(b) appeal if, in the year before the notification, the
child was already enrolled at a school **of that same direction** — regardless
of whether the child was yet of compulsory-school age, and even after a period
abroad. This is decisive in
[HR:2019:960](cases/ECLI-NL-HR-2019-960.md),
[HR:2022:1004](cases/ECLI-NL-HR-2022-1004.md) and
[PHR:2012:BV9201](cases/ECLI-NL-PHR-2012-BV9201.md). A 5(b) exemption is in
principle a **one-shot** entitlement at the start of compulsory schooling, not
something that can be acquired afresh for a later year.

### Procedural prerequisite
A valid appeal to 5(b) requires a prior **kennisgeving** (written notification)
to B&W stating that overwegende bedenkingen exist (art. 6/8). Without it, the
exemption cannot be invoked.

## 4. What decides the outcome — acquittal vs. conviction

The pivot is almost always requirement **(i)/(iii)**: is the worldview
*welbepaald* enough, and are the objections *concrete and weighty*, rather than
the 8(2) procedural bar.

**Acquittals (exemption accepted / charge not proven):**
- [GHARL:2024:2205](cases/ECLI-NL-GHARL-2024-2205.md) — **non-dualisme / Advaita
  Vedanta** accepted as a welbepaalde levensbeschouwing; objections concrete and
  weighty; all three requirements met → vrijspraak.
- [GHDHA:2021:505](cases/ECLI-NL-GHDHA-2021-505.md) — **Objectivisme**,
  thuisonderwijs; appeal court acquitted (kantonrechter had convicted, €1,000).
- [GHAMS:2018:1564](cases/ECLI-NL-GHAMS-2018-1564.md) — acquitted on final
  appeal after Hoge Raad referral.
- Several first-instance acquittals: [RBBRE:2010:BL7280](cases/ECLI-NL-RBBRE-2010-BL7280.md),
  [RBROT:2022:8514](cases/ECLI-NL-RBROT-2022-8514.md) /
  [8517](cases/ECLI-NL-RBROT-2022-8517.md).

**Convictions (objections rejected):**
- [HR:2018:1071](cases/ECLI-NL-HR-2018-1071.md) — **Holisme** rejected: not a
  *welbepaalde* religious/philosophical orientation; conviction upheld.
- [GHAMS:2024:2924](cases/ECLI-NL-GHAMS-2024-2924.md) — conviction; conditional
  fine €300.
- [GHAMS:2020:1670](cases/ECLI-NL-GHAMS-2020-1670.md) — conviction; fine €200.
- [RBGEL:2017:1420](cases/ECLI-NL-RBGEL-2017-1420.md) — conviction; €750 (€250
  conditional).
- [GHSHE:2012:BW4064](cases/ECLI-NL-GHSHE-2012-BW4064.md) — conviction;
  conditional fine €250.

**Cassation** ([HR](cases/) cases): mostly *verwerpt het beroep* (dismissed),
leaving the lower judgment intact; the value is the doctrinal reasoning above.
[PHR:2020:219](cases/ECLI-NL-PHR-2020-219.md) is an OM-cassatie against an
acquittal.

## 5. Sentencing in practice — far below the statutory ceiling

Across the convictions in the corpus, sentences cluster as **modest fines,
frequently (partly) conditional, with a 2-year proeftijd and a few days'
subsidiary hechtenis**. Custodial sentences are not imposed.

| Case | Imposed | Conditional? |
|---|---|---|
| [GHAMS:2020:1670](cases/ECLI-NL-GHAMS-2020-1670.md) | €200 | partly |
| [GHSHE:2012:BW4064](cases/ECLI-NL-GHSHE-2012-BW4064.md) | €250 (subs. 5 dagen) | fully conditional |
| [GHAMS:2024:2924](cases/ECLI-NL-GHAMS-2024-2924.md) | €300 (subs. 6 dagen) | fully conditional |
| [RBGEL:2017:1420](cases/ECLI-NL-RBGEL-2017-1420.md) | €750 | €250 conditional |
| [HR:2003:AF1264](cases/ECLI-NL-HR-2003-AF1264.md) | fl. 250–1,000 (pre-euro) | — |

**Takeaways on punishment:** even the heaviest fine seen (€750) is roughly a
sixth of the €4,500 statutory ceiling, and no one in the corpus received the
1-month hechtenis. The real "cost" of losing a richtingbezwaar case is not the
size of the fine but its **repeatability** (per child, per year) and the
underlying obligation to enrol the child.

> The €9,000 figure in [GHARL:2015:5814](cases/ECLI-NL-GHARL-2015-5814.md) is
> **not** an art. 26 criminal fine — that is a civil matter and a different
> track; it is included only as a peripheral, non-5(b) case.

## 6. One-paragraph summary

To escape compulsory-education prosecution under art. 5(b), a parent must show
serious conscientious objections rooted in a *well-defined* religion or
philosophy, aimed at the **direction** of *every* reachable school, that are
concrete and weighty — and must not already have placed the child at a school
of that direction (the art. 8(2) bar). Courts accept this for defined
worldviews like non-dualisme or Objectivisme, but reject loosely-defined ones
like "Holisme". The penalty is a minor-offence fine (statutory max ≈ €4,500 or
one month's hechtenis), but in practice fines run only €200–€750 and are often
conditional.
